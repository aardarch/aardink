# Spike: a diff's rows (0.12)

Status: **done**, 2026-11-22, on `feat/0.12`. This report records what the spike proved before the
diff editor in `docs/AARDINK_ROADMAP.md` §9.1 builds on it. Each step lists its evidence: a test in
the repository or a measurement.

**The question:** can a diff's rows (another document's deleted lines, and blank filler rows that
keep two sides aligned) be shown on 0.9's inline layout, side by side and inline, under soft wrap,
and at the size of real files, without a new layout mechanism?

The spike is internal to `:editor` and adds nothing public: `checkAbiAll` has nothing to report.

## What was built

- **A diff computer** (`core/diff/`): Myers's algorithm in linear space (the middle-snake divide and
  conquer), one core over `IntArray`s for lines and for characters. Lines are interned to ids, with
  leading and trailing whitespace trimmed when it is ignored, and a carriage return before a break
  never counts. A clock stops the search after `maxComputationTimeMs`. What is left then is one
  change per unresolved region, and `hitTimeout` says so. Character changes are worked out inside
  each change of at most 10,000 characters a side. `MinimalEdits`, which formatting and
  `replaceValue` use, now runs on the same core in place of its O(D²) trace.
- **Rows with a background.** `Injection` gains `background`, drawn under the text across the whole
  width of the rows it is on, and `spans`, styles over parts of its text, so that a deleted line is
  one injection in its own token colours. Several pieces per row would have stacked a translucent
  band.
- **A per-line table of a diff's rows on the state.** `publishDiffRows`, moved with edits as
  decorations are, is put first on its line by `InlineContent`, ahead of a lens. A row "before line
  n" of a document of n lines is injected at the end of its last line.
- **`DiffRows`**: filler rows line for line (`sideBySide`), filler rows by measured rows
  (`alignedByRows`), and deleted lines inline (`inline`).

## Found along the way

- **The budget a line's injected text is held to dropped a large deleted block.** A line may show
  10,000 characters of injected text, so that a long hint cannot cost what a long line is cut to
  avoid. A diff's rows are another document's lines, all of which must show: with the budget, 246 of
  2,000 deleted lines showed. Rows with a background are now outside it.
- **A large deleted block costs its first layout.** Every deleted line above one line is one
  paragraph. 10,000 of them took 178 ms to lay out on the JVM, and the browser is slower. Frames
  after that are cheap, because the layout is cached. Proposed: the inline view shows at most 1,000
  rows of a block, then a row saying how many more lines were deleted, which shows them all on a
  click.
- **Rows on both sides of one height make scrolling trivial.** Once filler rows have made the two
  sides the same number of rows, one `scrollY` and one `scrollX` serve both. Nothing maps one side's
  position to the other.
- **Under soft wrap, alignment is by rows, not lines.** Even an unchanged line wraps differently in
  panes of different widths, so every unchanged line can need filler rows. `alignedByRows` pairs
  every unchanged line with its other side's, and each block with its other side's. A line off
  screen is counted by the same estimate on both sides, so only lines coming on screen move
  anything, and they settle in the next pass.
- **A tab in a deleted line** becomes the spaces to its stop, as ghost text does: an injected tab
  has no stop of its own.

## What the units still have to deal with

- **Settling passes under soft wrap.** D5 republishes after each layout pass until nothing changes.
  The unit must do this from the layout pass of both sides, and limit it to the lines that changed
  rows, so that a scroll costs one extra pass at most.
- **An edit inside a change** drops the rows that were on the lines it replaced, until the diff is
  worked out again, 300 ms after typing stops as the gutter's lane is. Rows below the last line are
  dropped by an edit to that line.
- **Folding unchanged regions** (`hideUnchangedRegions`) was not tried here. The fold machinery
  already hides lines and rows. The unit must fold the matching regions on both sides at once, so
  that they keep one height.
- **The inline cap** above needs its row and its click.

## Decision

**Every step passes.** The diff editor needs no new layout mechanism: deleted lines and filler rows
are injected rows, as code lenses are. The proposed changes to §9.1 are:

| Feature | Rests on | Decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Side by side, line for line | D3, D6 | **Can ship** |
| Side by side under soft wrap, any split | D5 | **Can ship**, aligned by rows |
| Inline view | D1, D2, D4, D6 | **Can ship**, with at most 1,000 rows of a block shown before a row that shows the rest |
| Synchronised scrolling | D3 | **Can ship**, as one scroll position for both sides |
| The diff | Diff | **Can ship** |
